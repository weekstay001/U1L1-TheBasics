Welcome this site is about the main two cat breeds, Cobby and Oriental!

Overall information!

The main two breeds are Cobby cats and Oriental cats. Cobby cats have short, compact, and commonly heavy-boned bodies! I learned that a good example of this type of cat is the persian breed.

Oriental Cats on the other hand are extremely elongated and lean, their bodies often have very slim, long tails. An example of this type of cat is the Modern Siamese, which has a triangle-like head shape common for the breed type!
